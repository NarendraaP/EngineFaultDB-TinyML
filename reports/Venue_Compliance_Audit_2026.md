## 2. Detailed Venue Specifications (2026 Official Standards)

### 📄 Paper 1: IEEE Transactions on Computers (TC)
* **Publisher:** IEEE Computer Society
* **Article Type:** Regular Research Paper / Brief Contribution
* **Page Limits:** No hard cap on submission; standard allocation is 12 double-column pages before Mandatory Overlength Page Charges (MOPC) apply. Current 6-page manuscript is completely exempt from overlength fees.
* **Template:** IEEE Transactions double-column format (\documentclass[10pt,journal,compsoc]{IEEEtran} or standard [journal]).
* **Abstract:** 150–250 words, self-contained, no unexpanded acronyms or citations. (Current abstract: 218 words — compliant).
* **Keywords:** 4–8 IEEE taxonomy terms / Index Terms required. (Compliant).
* **Figures / Graphics:** Minimum 300 DPI for raster images, vector EPS/PDF preferred. (All current PNGs are 300 DPI — compliant).
* **Submission Portal:** ScholarOne Manuscripts (mc.manuscriptcentral.com/tc-ieee).
* **ORCID:** Required for corresponding author at submission.
* **Dual-Submission Policy:** Strict prohibition against simultaneous review elsewhere.

### 📄 Paper 2: IEEE Embedded Systems Letters (ESL) vs. ACM TODAES
* **Primary: IEEE Embedded Systems Letters (ESL)**
  * **Page Limit:** **Strict 4 pages maximum** (including all figures, tables, text, and references).
  * **Status:** Manuscript is currently 6 pages. Submitting directly to IEEE ESL requires condensing text, merging figures, and trimming references to reach exactly 4 pages.
* **Alternative: ACM Transactions on Design Automation of Electronic Systems (TODAES)**
  * **Page Limit:** Flexible; typical research contributions span 8–18 pages in ACM Primary Article Template.
  * **Status:** Current 6-page content can be formatted directly into ACM TODAES without content truncation.
* **Alternative: IEEE Transactions on Computer-Aided Design of Integrated Circuits and Systems (TCAD)**
  * **Page Limit:** Regular papers up to 12 pages; short papers up to 5 pages.
* **Recommendation:** If author prefers zero text cuts, submit to **ACM TODAES** or **IEEE TCAD**. If author prefers IEEE ESL's rapid turnaround, condense by 2 pages into an ultra-concise 4-page Letter.

### 📄 Paper 3: IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (TII)
* **Publisher:** IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES)
* **Article Type:** Regular Paper
* **Page Limit:** Initial submission must not exceed **10 double-column pages**. Overlength charges apply from page 11 onwards. (Current manuscript: 7 pages — perfectly compliant).
* **Template:** Standard IEEE Transactions format (IEEEtran.cls).
* **Scope & Fit:** Focuses on cyber-physical systems, intelligent diagnostics, sensor networks, and industrial edge computing. Paper 3's asymmetric engine monitoring cascade is a textbook fit for TII.
* **Submission Portal:** ScholarOne Manuscripts (mc.manuscriptcentral.com/tii).
* **Supplementary Material:** Code and benchmark datasets may be uploaded as IEEE Xplore multimedia / supplementary archives.

### 📄 Paper 4: IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering (TSE) / ACM TOSEM
* **Publisher:** IEEE Computer Society / ACM
* **Article Type:** Empirical Software Engineering Research Paper / Methodological Protocol
* **Page Limit:** Standard IEEE TSE papers span 12–18 pages. TSE and ACM TOSEM accept focused empirical studies.
* **Scope & Fit:** Addresses empirical verification, testing, and auditing of machine learning compilation pipelines (TFLite FlatBuffers, quantization, and data leakage).
* **Submission Portal:** ScholarOne Manuscripts (mc.manuscriptcentral.com/tse-cs).
* **Artifact Evaluation:** TSE and ACM TOSEM strongly encourage open-source reproducibility packages and artifact badges (Open Research Objects, Artifacts Evaluated & Reusable).
